TOPIC 1 - ROUTING 

Routing in front-end development is the process of navigating between different
pages or sections of a website without having to reload the entire page.
It helps users move from one page to another, such as Home, About, Contact,
or Profile, smoothly. In React, we commonly use React Router to manage routing.
It allows us to create multiple pages, connect them using links, and display
the correct content based on the URL. Routing makes websites easier to navigate, 
faster, and more user-friendly.

TOPIC - 2  TOAST NOTIFICATOIN 

Toast notification is a small pop-up message that appears on the screen to give
users quick feedback about an action they have performed. For example, when a user
successfully logs in, saves a form, deletes an item, or encounters an error, a toast 
notification displays a short message. It usually appears for a few seconds and 
disappears automatically. Toast notifications help users understand what is happening 
on a website without interrupting their work or navigation.
